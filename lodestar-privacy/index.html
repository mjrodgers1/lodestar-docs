Maker/checker and legal holds

Carried out, checked, recorded

Questions

Does personal data leave our Snowflake account?

No. The app runs in your account and keeps the identifiers there, only while a request is open. It shares only error and warning logs with us, and they never contain identifiers or values from your tables. See Telemetry.

Can erased data still be recovered?

For a while, by Snowflake's design: Time Travel keeps changed rows for the table's retention period, then Fail-safe for 7 more days (permanent tables). The evidence gives both dates for every table. See Evidence.

What doesn't it erase?

Anything outside the tables you register: clones, replicas, data shared to other accounts, files in stages, backups and exports, and other systems. The evidence report says so.
