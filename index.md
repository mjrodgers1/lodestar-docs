## Governed data changes inside your own Snowflake account

Lodestar Data Services is one simple, safe place for business and data teams to maintain the data
everything else depends on - customer segments, product and cost-centre codes, financial hierarchies,
country and currency codes, cross-system mappings, and your own control tables such as price lists and
thresholds.

Business users work in a familiar grid. Every change gets maker/checker approval, effective dating and a
complete audit trail, and is live in your queries as soon as it is approved (or on its effective date).
It is a Snowflake Native App: no servers, no connectors, no container compute, and your data stays in
your account.

### What you can do

- **Easy for business users**: edit many rows at once, paste rows copied from Excel, upload a CSV or
  Excel file, filter, and export to CSV or Excel. No spreadsheets passed around, no SQL.
- **Code sets, mappings and hierarchies** with typed attributes, validated on submit and on approval,
  and mapping suggestions - including AI similarity with Snowflake Cortex if you turn it on.
- **Your own tables, governed where they live**: inserts, updates and deletes go through approval and are
  then written to your table, with field-level history and one-click revert.
- **Maker/checker workflow**: stewards propose, admins approve; self-approval blocked by default;
  mandatory reasons and ticket references if you want them; comments and a *My work* queue.
- **Use it everywhere**: always-current views for SQL, pipelines and BI, a semantic view for Cortex
  Analyst, a Cortex Agent in Snowflake CoWork and an MCP server for AI tools - read-only, with each
  user's own access.
